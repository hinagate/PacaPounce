HOW

How AI intelligence becomes a paper trade

AI proposes; deterministic policy disposes
01 · ALPACA MCP

Session permission

Account identity, competition date, clock, orders, positions, and authorization decide whether a new AI proposal is permitted. A broker-side block prevents the Poe call.

02 · AI

Strategy intent

gemini-3.7-flash reads an Alpaca MCP brief with 1D/5D returns, RV20, nearest-ATM IV, IV/RV, and quote time, then returns direction, strategy, DTE, target delta, width, thesis, and invalidation. Missing data stays missing.

03 · VALIDATE

Strict intent schema

Unknown fields, unsupported structures, and incoherent values are rejected before a contract is priced.

04 · ALPACA MCP

Resolve contracts

Live chain, Greeks, OPRA bid/ask, bars, account, and positions determine the exact contracts and executable economics.

05 · POLICY

16 ordered gates

Every current gate runs without short-circuiting. One failure vetoes the entire proposal and all reasons remain visible.

06 · PAPER ONLY

Execute and monitor

run.py --loop owns both entry hunting and the deterministic risk monitor, including a restart-safe executable-profit ratchet, volatility response, re-entry lifecycle, and close-of-session cleanup.

Authority boundary. AI decides what idea to investigate and why. It never chooses an OCC symbol, trusts a price, calculates quantity, or sends an order. Alpaca data plus versioned policy decide the exact contract, whether it may trade, and how it exits.

02

Technology Implementation

Official Alpaca MCP server, stdio

MCP session supervisor · policy 1.5.0

Before Poe is called, the entry loop reconstructs permission from Alpaca. No daily trade counter or pending-order lock lives only in process memory.

A

Account identity

MCP

get_account_info.account_number must exactly match ALPACA_ACCOUNT_ID. Entry, monitor mutation, and financial presentation fail closed on mismatch; runtime evidence is account-scoped.

B

Regime-grounded AI

MCP + AI

get_stock_latest_quote, get_stock_bars, get_option_contracts, and get_option_snapshot create a timestamped brief of spot, 1D/5D returns, RV20, nearest-ATM IV, and IV/RV. It is cached for 300s; unavailable VIX or IV rank is never fabricated.

1

Trading session

MCP

get_clock + get_calendar pause outside the regular session, including shortened days. MCP next_open rolls the same process across nights, holidays, and weekends.

2

Pending-order lock

MCP

get_orders(status=open) blocks a new proposal while any opening or closing multi-leg order is unresolved.

3

Restart-safe count

MCP

get_orders(status=all) groups parent PacaPounce entries and chase revisions; get_account_activities(FILL) corroborates child-leg execution without counting two legs as two trades.

4

Live exposure

MCP

get_all_positions reconstructs option spreads and held symbols instead of trusting a local ledger.

5

Options authorization

MCP

get_account_info must report ACTIVE, unblocked, approved and enabled at Level 3+, with positive options buying power.

6

Full-capital sizing

MCP

The largest integer quantity whose complete defined loss fits inside live options_buying_power is computed, gated, and checked again immediately before submission.

7

Full-capital wait

MCP

Once a full-buying-power spread is confirmed open, entry hunting pauses before Poe is called while risk monitoring continues.

8

Fill-aware repricing

MCP + POLICY

An unfilled opening order improves by at most one cent per monitor cycle, never below the live natural credit. Before replacement, its reserved defined loss is released in preflight; after cancellation, get_account_info must confirm enough options BP. EV is recomputed at the actual replacement limit, without charging the bid/ask concession twice. An MCP accepted envelope is not success: get_orders(status=all) must return the same client ID and an Alpaca broker order ID.

9

Hold to expiry

POLICY

The entry gate prices the spread on its terminal payoff, so the monitor delivers that payoff: no profit target and no trail. The spread is held to expiry and closed at market only if the underlying crosses the long strike; the final-30-minute pin-risk exit still applies. Disabled profit exits are recorded as hold_to_expiry in the session log.

10

Better re-entry

MCP + POLICY

Profit exits wait 15 minutes plus 10 calm, liquid minutes. A new pair must improve post-cost EV/risk by 0%, worsen no risk dimension, and uses 100% of options BP. Risk exits lock the session.

Second-strategy MCP lane

S1

One bounded scan

MCP + QUANT

get_stock_bars supplies adjusted daily and completed 15-minute SIP bars. Python computes SMA200, Wilder RSI(2), EMA5, ATR14, ranking, quantity, and stop exactly once at 15:45 ET on normal sessions.

S2

Auditable event review

MCP + AI

get_news supplies timestamped articles for the selected numerical candidate. Poe may veto concrete event risk and write the thesis. The feed is not mislabeled as a verified earnings calendar.

S3

Exact long-call execution

MCP

get_option_chain resolves a liquid 14-30 DTE call near 0.70 delta. place_option_order submits a buy-to-open limit; get_orders must confirm it before SUBMITTED.

S4

Deterministic lifecycle

MCP + POLICY

The 30-second monitor reconciles broker option positions, checks the underlying 2×ATR14 stop, and sends a sell-to-close limit on stop, EMA5 recovery, or session three. The LLM never times exits.

Current entry stack: 16 gates, one by one

Intent parsing, coherence validation, and contract construction happen first. A fully resolved candidate then passes through every gate below in this exact order; evaluation never stops at the first failure.

Version transparency. The current stack is gate 1.4.0. Historical live entries were recorded under gate 1.0.0 with 12 checks; 1.1.0 added the annual-target budget, and 1.2.0 added broker-owned options eligibility plus options-buying-power sizing. Gate 1.3.0 added symmetric call-tail economics and the call-rebound guard. Historical detail marks later checks not recorded instead of retroactively claiming they ran.
The gate was tested against outcomes, not asserted. The harness scores the trades the gate rejected as well as the ones it approved - the counterfactual every naive version skips. Across 12,000 independent opportunities, approved trades averaged $8.23 and vetoed trades -$0.47, a separation of $8.70 per trade at p=0.0040. Reproduce with python scripts/validate_gate.py --trials 12000.

Why an economic gate at all

Total P&L by policy across the same 12,000 simulated opportunities. Operational rules alone (defined risk, size caps, liquidity, limit orders) prevent malformed trades, not unprofitable ones — they can score worse than no filter because they reject trades for being large, not underpaid.
